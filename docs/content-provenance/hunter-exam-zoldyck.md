# Hunter Exam: the visit to Killua's home

Part of #760. This part covers the visit within 2011 episodes 22–26 and manga
chapters 39–43. Episode 25 also adapts chapter 44; the departure plans and later
destinations from that chapter are deliberately excluded. The 1999 anime is not
a source. English Hunterpedia pages were read through the lawful MediaWiki API
on 2026-10-26. The cited chapter and episode summaries are evidence for facts,
not claims of directly viewed dialogue or panels.

## Places and dated changes

- **Republic of Padokea, 22 / 39:** filed at arrival, after its map/mention in
  21 / 38. The country is visited on the way to Killua, independently of the
  mountain. Source:
  [Republic of Padokea](https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_of_Padokea).
- **Kukuroo Mountain, 22 / 39:** filed at the visit, after the earlier mention.
  The Testing Gate, service door, guard quarters, butler quarters and family
  house remain landmarks within this parent place. Dentora is the geographic
  district of the country, not a separate stop or new global region label.
  Source:
  [Kukuroo Mountain](https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Kukuroo_Mountain).
- The Italian geographic forms **Repubblica di Padokia** and **Monte Kukruu**
  are editorial forms following Italian Wikipedia's geographical usage; they are
  not represented as directly checked 2011 dialogue.
- Killua's disqualification is pinned at **20 / 36**, his and Illumi's family
  home links at **22 / 39**, and Killua's departure with Gon at **25 / 43**. No
  later profession, destination or passing result is inferred for him. Source:
  chapters 36, 39 and 43 and their 2011 counterparts.
- Affiliations stay prose. There are no One Piece factions, bounty fields or new
  HxH sorting groups.
