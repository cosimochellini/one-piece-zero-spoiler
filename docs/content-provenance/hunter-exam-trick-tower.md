# Hunter Exam: airship and Trick Tower

Part of #760, following #829. Researched on 2026-10-26 through Hunterpedia’s
MediaWiki API and Italian Wikipedia. Coverage: 2011 episodes 7–12 and manga
chapters 13–22, ending before the Fourth Phase. Episodes 13 and 26 are recaps;
Trick Tower ends in episode 12, rather than episode 11 as the earlier issue
comment suggested. All prose and SVG geometry are original.

## Evidence limits and human checks before merge

The wiki narrative and infoboxes were read; licensed manga pages and episodes
were **not viewed directly**. Human checks: exact pages/captions of Lippo,
Bendot and Sedokan’s names; their episode-8 naming timing; the Italian 2011 dub
forms absent from Wikipedia; every drawn object’s precise appearance in both
sources. Sedokan’s drawing uses his discarded shroud, not the candles whose
anime challenge starts in episode 9. No facial features, tattoos, official
symbols, lettering or Nen are drawn. Togari’s knives are anime-only artwork. The
original geometry is not traced. No reference scans, frames, dialogue, subtitles
or images are committed. Distinctive-design approval remains with the owner,
following #761’s drawing review policy.

Beans, applicants and the later phases are reconciled by the other #760 parts;
this phase adds none of their identities. The unnamed third examiner, flying
beast species and anonymous rock climber do not receive proper-name records.
