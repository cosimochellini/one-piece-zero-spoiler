## Evidence and editorial policy

Sources are One Piece Wiki contributors. Revision-pinned pages below and the
source inventory record the reviewed factual references and the SHA-256 of
retrieved revision wikitext. Types and explanations are original IT/EN writing,
with no adapted passages, dialogue, scans, frames or new artwork.

Selections favor lasting personal ties, established working partnerships and
defining encounters. A later snapshot replaces the full editorial selection;
omissions do not assert a relationship ended. Unchanged people retain their
earlier wording and history. Later flashbacks may justify new selections without
implying the source character learned revelations after their death. Ace’s Sabo
card describes their childhood oath, never knowledge of Sabo’s survival.

Coverage stops at the base archive, including the 1180/1150 giant
transformations already represented by its redrawings. Later recovery material
in current wiki pages is excluded. The bookmark ceiling of 1300 is not a story
cutoff.
