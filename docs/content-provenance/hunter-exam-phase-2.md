## Evidence limits

The sources above are wiki narrative and infoboxes, not direct viewing of
licensed pages or episodes. Hanzo's and Nicholas's names in Tonpa's recalled
encounters, the precise page of each name and the drawing objects need human
verification before merge. Every new drawing's manga scene is unviewed; only
Hisoka's initial 2011 design was viewed directly. The contact sheets are
generated locally with `art:sheet`. No green tint relies on an unchecked anime
colour.

Menchi's knives are not used: chapter 12 shows them after her chapter 10 gate.
Netero's later ball is not used. Hisoka has no face, suit symbols or later
powers. The shared logs omit the manga-only sushi test and the anime-only
updraft method. No later outcome is added to an earlier frozen log.
