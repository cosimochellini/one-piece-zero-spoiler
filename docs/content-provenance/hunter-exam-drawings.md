# Hunter Exam: the drawing pass

Part of #761. Research date: 2026-10-26. Uses the English Hunterpedia MediaWiki
API
(`https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/api.php?action=parse&prop=wikitext&format=json&page=…`).
Licensed manga pages and episode footage were not viewed: every object below is
checked against the wiki's text, and the frames remain a human check before
merge, as for #807 and #829. No wiki wording, frames, scans or reference images
are committed.

## Scope

The 45 characters filed with the Hunter Exam (Ging, Kite and Kon among them,
though the 2011 anime first shows them later), its 10 places and
`hunter-exam-arc`, graded against the `line-drawings` skill. 39 drawings graded
B or C were redrawn; the other 17 were graded A and kept. The later arcs are
unchanged.

identity mark only. Hanzo's comment no longer says his hidden blade comes "much

stays out of the drawing. Menchi keeps her hair rope: the sushi is chapter 11
and her knives chapter 12, after her chapter 10 threshold.

A wiki sweep of the 56 records found no other stage between episode 1 and 26,
chapter 1 and 43: no other death after a record opens, no costume, lost object
or damaged place. Johness, Togari and Bourbon die in the episode their record
opens (the skill's exception). Hanzo's blade is a one-fight weapon. Injuries
(Gon's arm, Illumi's wrist, Canary) are states of the body.
