# Hunter Exam: Fourth Phase and final bracket

Part of #760. Research date: 2026-10-26. Uses the English Hunterpedia MediaWiki
API and Italian Wikipedia. Episode ranges refer to the 2011 anime; the 1999
series and its additional exam phase are excluded. Source summaries were read;
licensed manga pages and episode footage were not viewed. Every new drawing and
exact naming scene below remains a human check before merge, following the
policy used for #807 and #829. No wiki wording, dialogue, subtitles, frames,
scans or reference images are committed.

## Rights and evidence limits

All dossier prose and path geometry were written independently. Drawing objects
are supported by the character pages' Appearance sections, not by viewed
licensed scenes: the exact cap seams, headset, robe, hat flaps and needle shapes
require human verification. No green tint is justified from an unviewed frame.
Distinctive designs remain subject to the owner's pre-merge art approval under
#761. Generated art sheets and browser screenshots stay outside commits.
