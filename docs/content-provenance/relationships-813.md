## Evidence and selection policy

Sources are One Piece Wiki contributors. Revision metadata and SHA-256 hashes of
retrieved revision wikitext are in `sources.json`; the links below pin the
reviewed revisions. Only factual claims are used. Types and explanations are
original IT/EN writing, not copied passages, translations or dialogue. No
artwork or reference images are added.

The table inventories every complete selection in editorial order. Its episode
and chapter links provide the event or the reached story context. Unchanged
retained wording uses the evidence from its earlier stage; a new selection does
not assert that an omitted person’s relationship ended. Selection order favours
narrative relevance and carries forward without saga resets. Coverage is through
the base archive’s reached Elbaf revelations, not the bookmark form ceiling
of 1300.

Initial Luffy selections differ by source because chapter 1 introduces Shanks
before Koby, while the anime places that flashback in episode 4. Zoro’s
selections likewise respect Kuina’s existing chapter-53 endpoint. Those
source-specific entries deliberately do not anchor the shared chapter mapping.
Their other-unit number is an ordering reference, not a claim of simultaneous
disclosure.

Existing endpoint gates delay several otherwise earlier facts:
Mohji/Richie/Cabaji to chapter 21, Kuina to chapter 53, Garp to episode
313/chapter 431 (the training selection is filed at the 314/432 reunion),
Camie/Pappag to chapter 493, and the sea creature to episode 504/chapter 589.
These endpoint delays are not proposed corrections to the underlying archive.

Koby’s original 1/2, 315/433 and 488/579 prose is retained from
`relationships-812.md`, with Helmeppo/Garp selections and the 1122/1088 rescue
added. Mansherry remains unchanged and owned by Dressrosa.

No unsupported romance, reciprocal relationship or latest-wiki revelation beyond
the base archive is inferred. Shanks’s family is shared at 1168/1138: chapter
1137 establishes Shamrock’s father, and 1138 establishes the twins. The
1156/1126 confrontation does not claim Bartolomeo died. Sanji’s farewell wording
reports Pudding removing his memory; it does not imply he remembers it.
