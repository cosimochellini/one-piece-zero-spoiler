Part of #760. Facts checked on 2026-10-26 through Hunterpedia’s MediaWiki API
and Italian Wikipedia. Coverage: 2011 episodes 19–21 and manga chapters 33–38.
Original wording and SVG geometry; no source images, dialogue or scans are
committed.

Gittarackur and Illumi are separate records, as with the existing One Piece
disguise records. Their connection is stated only once Illumi opens. The
disguise record receives no early true-name alias. Italian Wikipedia writes the
disguise as Ghitarakur and the revealed brother as Illumi Zoldick; the safe
short name is Illumi.

Opening summaries and logs remain frozen at their named-scene gates. Subsequent
exam outcomes extend dated affiliations rather than rewriting early prose.
Killua does not receive a licensed affiliation.

## Exclusions

Beans is not an unresolved early naming gate: Hunterpedia’s Trivia section
explicitly dates the manga name Beans to chapter 319. Mamen comes from the 1999
anime’s credits and a databook; Mamen Beans is supplemental material. Neither
qualifies within this task’s chapter-43 in-story naming limit. The official can
remain unnamed in place/exam prose.

The Final Phase hotel has no supported proper name in the checked chapters; no
invented place record is added. No later Nen abilities, later surnames or
outcomes after the coverage are added.
