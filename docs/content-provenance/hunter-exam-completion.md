# Hunter Exam completion ledger

Delivery of #760 is split into five dependent PRs: source-aware shelves and
chapter-one records; Airship/Trick Tower; Zevil and named finalists; Final
Phase; the visit to Killua's home. Merge in that order; the PRs track the
current merge and review state.

The stack grows Hunter x Hunter from 29 records (16 characters, six places,
seven arcs) to 62 records (45 character/identity records, ten places, seven
arcs). Gittarackur and Illumi are intentionally separate gated identity records,
following the existing One Piece disguise pattern. Their connection is dated at
episode 20 / chapter 35; no early true-name alias is sent.

## Coverage and exclusions

- Shared facts stay within 2011 episodes 1–26 and manga chapters 1–43. Episode
  25's chapter-44 departure plans are excluded. Recaps add no records.
- Kite and Kon are included because chapter one is inside the manga coverage;
  their anime naming scenes open at episode 76. Both are unanchored and keep
  explicit chapter pins. Manga shelves use the Hunter Exam heading without
  changing valid One Piece shelf membership.
- Named animals qualify. Species, descriptive creature titles, unnamed extras,
  unvisited places, and names known only from supplemental material do not.
- Beans is out of this span: his manga name is revealed in chapter 319; Mamen is
  from 1999 credits/databook material. No early record is invented.
- Remaining applicants and earlier candidate naming checks are finite follow-up
  [#833](https://github.com/cosimochellini/zero-spoiler/issues/833).
- Kikyo, Silva and Hishita's positive personal-name checks are follow-up
  [#834](https://github.com/cosimochellini/zero-spoiler/issues/834).
- Every part's provenance matrix records its individual evidence and precise
  human checks. Unviewed licensed scenes, uncertain exact naming pages and dub
  forms are not presented as directly verified. Owner art approval remains
  [#761](https://github.com/cosimochellini/zero-spoiler/issues/761); chronicle
  stories remain
  [#762](https://github.com/cosimochellini/zero-spoiler/issues/762).

## Dossiers and interfaces

Opening summaries and logs stay safe at the first named gate. Significant exam
results, deaths, identity disclosures and home/departure facts are dated.
Origins link to filed places when supported. Affiliations remain prose; no HxH
group taxonomy, bounties or One Piece faction filters were added. Nested
gate/estate/quarters locations remain landmarks within the parent mountain.

Each new record has an original first drawing; Bodoro's later death has a gated
redrawing. Records already dead when named use the existing same-gate exception.
No later Nen, costumes, weapons or outcomes are added. All public payload and
bookmark shapes remain unchanged.
