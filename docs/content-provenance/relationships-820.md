# Dressrosa relationship coverage

Checked on 2026-10-27 for #820 against base
`1222bfe2c82ae80b308dbc6923428efbdd4d4458`.

The debut module contains 63 characters. All 63 have supported directed links;
there are no no-link exclusions. Mansherry’s existing pilot stays inline,
unchanged. The other 62 characters have 216 complete dated selections. Coverage
stops at the base archive’s episode 1180 / chapter 1150, not the bookmark form’s
larger ceiling or later wiki revelations.

## Evidence and selection policy

Sources are One Piece Wiki contributors. The inventory below links the reviewed
character revisions and episode/chapter revisions. Retrieved revision ids,
timestamps and SHA-256 hashes of revision wikitext are added to `sources.json`
without changing the earlier audits’ pinned entries or metadata. The wiki is
used for factual research only. Types and explanations are independently written
in IT/EN; no source passages, dialogue, scans, frames or new artwork are
included.

Each list is a complete selection in editorial order, not an inferred reciprocal
graph. Four or five is a cap and target, never a quota. Defining personal bonds
and reached story relevance guide the order. A removed card does not assert that
the relationship ended. Unchanged cards reuse identical prose and do not add a
history stage. The last reached selection carries forward indefinitely.

Episode/chapter numbers in the table are publication gates. They can be later
than the earliest evidence because both character endpoints must already be
open. The linked character revision supplies the earlier event’s chapter/episode
citations where the gate is delayed. Earlier relationship facts are never
backdated past an endpoint just because the target is named in other prose.

## Source-specific and delayed disclosures

- Rebecca’s mother is told at 651/721, but Scarlett’s existing manga endpoint is
  chapter 742. The anime-only 651/721 and 659/727 lists retain Scarlett; the
  manga 659/727 list omits her. Both converge at the verified family revelation
  675/742. Scarlett’s own source-specific lists likewise wait behind her
  endpoint and the anime’s later flashback; her anime chapter numbers are
  ordering references, not assertions of an additional manga event.
- Kyros’s family opens at 675/742. References to him as a known person wait for
  that endpoint even when earlier scenes show the unidentified toy soldier.
- Koala’s childhood evidence is
  [chapter 622](https://onepiece.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=1947419) and episode
  541, held behind her existing chapter-626 endpoint. Kin’emon and Momonosuke’s
  cover story is stated as claimed parentage, then replaced at 768/817 when the
  retainer relationship is explicitly revealed. Oden’s own endpoint delays his
  cards until 960/960 or later.
- Rosinante’s existing chapter-768 endpoint delays references to his earlier
  actions: Vergo’s attack is verified at
  [705](https://onepiece.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=2021373) /
  [766](https://onepiece.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=2108747); the Barrels theft
  is told at 704/765 and his murder at 706/767. Their authored gates wait until
  chapter 768.
- [SBS volume 76](https://onepiece.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=2092132)
  establishes that Jora raises Dellinger.
  [Volume 76](https://onepiece.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=2108749) ends at
  chapter 763, used conservatively as the manga-only gate. The episode 702
  number is an ordering reference; it does not claim this supplemental
  disclosure is in the anime. Later Jora and Dellinger selections remain split
  by source to preserve this known link.
- [SBS volume 77](https://onepiece.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=2000738)
  establishes Monet and Sugar as sisters.
  [Volume 77](https://onepiece.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=2122564) ends at
  chapter 775, used conservatively as the manga-only gate. Episode 715 is an
  ordering reference. No biological relationship is inferred merely from their
  crew membership.
- Sai and Baby 5’s wedding is the
  [chapter-881 cover](https://onepiece.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=1947705),
  rather than the story adapted in episode 853. The marriage is manga-only; the
  anime keeps their engagement state. Sai’s later 1118/1084 selection is
  authored separately for each source so the wedding is neither leaked nor
  forgotten.
- Caesar and Judge establish NEO MADS on the
  [chapter-1078 cover](https://onepiece.fandom.com/index.php?oldid=2099464).
  This is manga-only; episode 1110 supplies only an ordering reference.
- Sugar faints at 676/742; the text at that gate does not reveal the next
  episode/chapter’s restoration of the toys. Kanjuro’s final fire waits for
  1055/1030, independently of his earlier apparent defeat.
- Bartolomeo’s 1156/1126 selection reports his refusal to betray Luffy; it does
  not assert his death. Hajrudin’s family and Road’s childhood friendship are
  checked against 1160/1130 and 1167/1137, with his resentment updated at
  1173/1143. Later wiki accounts of reconciliation are outside this base
  archive.

## Deliberate omissions

Only existing character records can be endpoints. Señor Pink’s wife Russian and
son Gimlet, Sai’s former fiancée Uholisia, Cavendish’s horse Farul, Koala’s
mother, and other unrecorded people are not introduced through this issue. Their
absence from the cards does not mean that their bonds are unsupported. Hakuba is
an alter ego of Cavendish, not a separate person card. Factions and
organisations are not endpoints. No romance is inferred for Viola/Sanji,
Leo/Mansherry or Koala/Sabo. The Sai/Baby 5 engagement and manga marriage are
explicitly sourced. The grouped Rock-and-Scotch record and existing animal
characters retain the project’s established character-endpoint treatment.
