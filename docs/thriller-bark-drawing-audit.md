# Thriller Bark drawing audit (#473)

This checklist covers the 20 character records first filed in Thriller Bark, in
character-ID order. Episode/chapter pairs are written **episode / chapter**.
Existing stages are inventory, not newly verified frame evidence. The baseline
is the existing `thrillerBarkArt` and `thrillerBarkRedrawn` data; its drawings
and purification stages are preserved.

The drawing rules require manga-frame checks to establish geometry, start and
end. Wiki prose, gallery captions and Qref citations identify leads; they do not
establish a complete temporary-stage interval. A checked inventory row means the
record was considered, not that every later manga panel was inspected. This
audit does not certify that no further eligible drawing exists.

## Candidate decisions and open evidence

### Perona: miniature Kumashi, candidate 522 / 602

The [appearance section](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Perona#Appearance)
describes her retained parasol and small Kumashi-like stuffed animal, citing
**522 / 602**, pp. 4, 13. The small bear also appears in a historical flashback
in chapter **468** (episode **363**); it must not be described as newly acquired
after the timeskip. Historical possession alone does not prove the object is
carried continuously in present-day scenes.

The same source records an adaptation difference at **917 / 925**: the manga has
the full-sized zombie body, while the anime uses the miniature plush. That scene
cannot establish a matching manga/anime plush interval without further checks.

**Deferred.** Manga pages **601–602**, episode **521–522**, and later scenes
establishing loss/return have not been frame-checked for this audit. A valid
temporary stage needs both its start and return, reusing the original parasol
drawing. Do not ship an indefinite plush stage based only on an appearance
summary.

### Ryuma: Shusui returned to grave, candidate 932 / 936

The [Shusui history](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Shusui#History) is a lead
for the reported return. It does not establish which grave details are visible
at that moment. The wiki's later grave image is associated with episode
**1046**; using its geometry at **932** would require earlier-frame evidence.
Keep the sword-exchange discussion and later grave imagery distinct from the
report of return.

**Deferred.** Manga **935–936**, episode **931–932**, and the later grave scene
have not been frame-checked to establish a faithful composition at the candidate
threshold. Keep the existing **362 / 467** ash drawing.

### Risky Brothers: returned shadows, accepted 375 / 483

[Spoil's Qref](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Spoil) explicitly pairs the
collective restoration with **375 / 483**. The Risky Brothers' own citations
establish their shadowless introduction and later survival, but do not by
themselves show the ground beneath their objects.

**Accepted and implemented.** Chapter **483**, “The End of the Dream,” shows
Lola and the Risky Brothers beginning to vanish before the shadows return, then
the Rolling Pirates celebrating with their shadows in the ground-level group
panel (scan image **007**, middle panel). The inspected
[reference page](https://onepiece20465.blogspot.com/2019/11/one-piece-chapter-482.html)
is mislabeled **482** in its URL; its chapter heading explicitly identifies
**483**. The image, not the URL label, establishes the chapter.
[Episode 375](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_375) independently
describes Lola and her crew celebrating the restoration;
[episode 374](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_374) ends with the
shadowless victims beginning to burn. `wiki:pairs` confirms chapter **482** maps
to **374** and **483** to **375**.

The preceding chapter’s final spread was also inspected: victims are still
vanishing while Moria releases the shadows
([reference page](https://onepiece20465.blogspot.com/2019/11/one-piece-chapter-481.html),
actual chapter **482**, despite its URL).

The stage reuses every stroke of the sword and striped shirt, adding only the
house-style ground shadow. It preserves their tint and identity accents. The
return of their shadows is permanent, so no exit entry is needed. Regression
coverage checks episode **374/375**, chapter **482/483**, no bookmark and
preservation of the original objects.

### Brook: later headwear and disguises

The [main-series gallery](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Brook/Gallery)
explicitly identifies a new Zou hat and coat, alongside post-timeskip, Whole
Cake Island, Wano, Egghead and Elbaph outfits, a Luffy-duplicate disguise and a
Beasts Pirates disguise. These are research leads. Readable headwear or an
arc-long disguise can qualify even when the violin/guitar stays the same.

**Unresolved, not categorically excluded.** Gallery captions do not give
verified scene boundaries. Main-series image frames and their manga counterparts
have not been checked here for the Zou hat, the disguises or later headwear; no
reliable start/return pairs are established. Elbaph manga-only looks require a
verified adapted episode before consideration. Exclude gallery sections for
filler, films, openings/endings, games and promotional art. A body state or
one-fight technique alone does not qualify. Preserve **517 / 598** without
representing it as the final possible Brook stage.

## Status and verification record

Existing deaths, presumed death and purification drawings stay intact. A zombie
being identified as already dead is not a second death composition; its later
purification remains a separate existing stage. No newly verified missing death
or presumed-death event is established by this document, and Yorki's unknown
fate is not changed.

This is a source inventory with explicit deferrals. It records neither a clean
test run nor grade-A visual approval. The implementation PR must separately
report any accepted stage's exact frame evidence, before/after episode and
chapter checks, no-bookmark behavior, rendered crest/tile review, archive
mapping diff, duplicate-aware dated-entry counts and the completed
`npm run check` result. Scratch caches and reference images are not
deliverables.
