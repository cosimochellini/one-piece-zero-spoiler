# Status episodes to verify

This file lists the `status` entries whose episode number was filed from memory
rather than from something the archive already stated. It is a review aid, not
part of the site, and can be deleted once the numbers are checked.

Everything **not** listed here is safe by construction: an entry filed at the
character's own `revealedAtEpisode` takes its number from the record, and
nineteen further entries took theirs from an affiliation line that already named
the same episode (Hiluluk 85, Rosinante 704, Ginny 1117, Otohime 539, Fisher
Tiger 539, Rouge 493, Yorki 380, Orochi 1085, Xebec 958, Toki 963, Higurashi
963, Semimaru 963, Sora 799, Carmel 836, Vergo 613, Vegapunk 1116, Hody Jones
574, Caribou 574, Sabo 497).

The direction of a mistake matters: an episode filed **too early** shows a fate
to a reader who has not reached it, which is the one thing the site exists to
prevent. An episode filed too late only withholds it for a while. Where unsure,
these were rounded up rather than down.

Two editorial calls are worth a second opinion rather than a number:

- **Characters the story introduces in a flashback and then kills inside it**
  (Kuina, Bell-mere) are filed `deceased` from their own threshold, which is
  what the archive already does for Otohime, Rouge and Toki. It shows the fate a
  few episodes before the scene that delivers it. Moving each to the episode of
  the death would be more precise and less consistent.
- **`captured` vs `imprisoned`**: held by someone right now versus serving a
  sentence. Caribou is `captured`, Hody Jones `imprisoned`.
